# Colorado News Sources

Primary news sources for documenting ICE incidents in Colorado.

## Tier 1: Major Daily Newspapers

### The Denver Post
- **Type:** Daily newspaper
- **Coverage Area:** Denver metro, statewide
- **URL:** https://www.denverpost.com/
- **Immigration Coverage:** Extensive coverage of ICE operations, legal challenges
- **Notes:** Daily circulation ~57,000; Colorado's largest paper

### Colorado Sun
- **Type:** Online nonprofit
- **Coverage Area:** Statewide
- **URL:** https://coloradosun.com/
- **Immigration Coverage:** Excellent investigative reporting on ICE arrests, data analysis
- **Notes:** Founded 2018 by former Denver Post journalists; data-driven reporting

### Denver Gazette
- **Type:** Online news site
- **Coverage Area:** Denver metro
- **URL:** https://www.denvergazette.com/
- **Notes:** Owned by Philip Anschutz, affiliated with Gazette and Colorado Politics

## Key Communities

Colorado's immigrant population is concentrated in:
- **Denver:** Largest city, sanctuary city, highly publicized enforcement
- **Aurora:** Large immigrant population, long-time home of refugees, apartment raids in February 2025
- **Colorado Springs:** Growing community, nightclub raid in April 2025
- **Greeley/Weld County:** Agricultural workforce
- **Western Slope:** Agricultural and service workers

## Recent Enforcement Context

- **2025 arrest totals:** 3,500+ arrests (4x increase from Biden era)
- **Denver arrests:** 5x increase since Trump took office (~500/month vs ~140/month)
- **February 2025:** Aurora/Denver apartment raids
- **April 2025:** Colorado Springs nightclub raid (72 arrests in one day)
- **November 2025:** Federal judge rules ICE conduct "unlawful," issues injunction
- **Criminal records:** Only 30% of July 2025 arrests involved criminal prosecution
- **ICE expansion:** Agency nearly doubled headcount in CO; 3 new detention centers planned
