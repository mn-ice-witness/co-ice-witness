# ICE Arrests 243 People in 9-Day Denver Metro Operation

## Summary
U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement conducted a 9-day enforcement operation across the Denver metro area from July 12-20, 2025, arresting 243 people. ICE claimed all those arrested were charged with or convicted of criminal offenses, though critics noted the operation reflected a significant escalation of arrests in Colorado, with the state seeing more than 17 arrests per day during this period.

## Official Accounts

### ICE Statement
Robert Guadian, ICE Enforcement and Removal Operations Denver Field Office Director: "This operation is a testament to our unwavering commitment to ensuring the safety and security of our communities... We will continue to work tirelessly to identify and apprehend those who pose a threat to public safety."

ICE noted that many of those arrested had been "previously released into the Denver metro area by local county jails" under Colorado's sanctuary laws that limit ICE cooperation.

## Context
This operation represented a significant escalation in ICE activity in Colorado. Between June 11 and July 28, 2025, ICE arrested 828 people in Colorado, averaging more than 17 arrests per day. This was a more than 50% increase from the first five months of the Trump administration.

ICE arrests in Colorado peaked in July 2025, with 548 arrests that month alone. However, Denver Post analysis found that people detained in summer 2025 were less likely to have criminal backgrounds than those arrested earlier in the year.

By year's end, data showed that only about 30% of ICE arrests in Colorado involved individuals with any prior criminal convictions.
