# ICE Agent Fires 3 Shots at Vehicle During Black Forest Raid

## Summary
ICE agents raided the Timber Ridge housing development construction site in Black Forest, north of Colorado Springs, seeking two fugitives. When suspects allegedly attempted to drive into a federal agent, an ICE officer fired three shots at the vehicle. No injuries were reported. One suspect was arrested; the other (Jose Mendez-Chavez) remained at large for months. The El Paso County Sheriff's Office issued a shelter-in-place order during the operation. Construction workers from multiple job sites fled when ICE arrived. Activists disputed claims that the men tried to ram ICE vehicles.

## Official Accounts

### ICE Statement
Claimed suspects attempted to "ram" federal officers with their vehicle, justifying the use of deadly force.

### Colorado Immigrant Rights Coalition
CIRC demanded an investigation, disputing ICE's account of events. Activists questioned whether deadly force was justified.

### Community Impact
A 16-year-old girl came to the site after receiving video from her uncle showing a fellow worker being detained while others ran. Witnesses reported 5-6 people detained. Family members expressed concern the site was targeted because the construction company was owned by Mexican immigrants.
