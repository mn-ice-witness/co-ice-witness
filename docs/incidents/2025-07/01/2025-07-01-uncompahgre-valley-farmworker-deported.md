# Father of Five Deported Without Hearing; Daughter Has Leukemia

## Summary
A Mexican father of five who had worked in the Uncompahgre Valley farm fields was detained by ICE while on his way to work. He was quickly deported without a hearing. He had three minor traffic offenses and was driving without a license. His U.S. citizen daughter reportedly has leukemia and he had been driving her to Denver for treatment. The deportation left the family without a primary caregiver for the sick child.

## Sources

- **Outcome:** Deported without hearing

## Context
The Uncompahgre Valley in western Colorado is an agricultural region dependent on immigrant labor. While few ICE operations have been confirmed in the valley's farm fields, fear among farmworkers is reported to be at unprecedented levels.

## Impact
The deportation left a U.S. citizen child with cancer without her father, who had been responsible for driving her to Denver for treatment.
