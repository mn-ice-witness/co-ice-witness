# Aurora Police and ICE Joint Operation Detains Bystanders in Freezing Temperatures

## Summary
Aurora Police Department conducted a joint operation with ICE at The Edge at Lowry apartments, handcuffing all residents present including children as young as 3 and 8 years old. Residents were held outside in 28°F weather in their pajamas from approximately 3:00 AM to 4:30 AM. Children were separated from parents and placed in separate patrol cars. No warrants were shown despite requests. Some people were held for up to 8 hours. The ACLU filed a lawsuit accusing Aurora Police of violating state immigration cooperation laws.

## Timeline
- **~3:00 AM** - Joint APD/ICE operation begins at Edge at Lowry apartments
- Agents handcuff all residents present, including minor children
- Residents held outside in freezing temperatures (28°F) wearing pajamas
- Children separated from parents and placed in separate patrol cars
- No warrants shown despite repeated requests from residents and legal observers
- No food, water, or restrooms provided for extended period
- Some residents forced to urinate in patrol cars
- **~4:30 AM** - Outdoor detention ends but questioning continues
- Some people detained for up to 8 hours
- Questioning focused heavily on immigration status rather than criminal investigation

## Official Accounts

### Aurora Police Department
APD participated in joint operation with federal agents. Handcuffed all residents present as part of standard procedure.

### ACLU of Colorado
Filed lawsuit accusing Aurora Police of violating Colorado state law limiting local law enforcement cooperation with federal immigration officers. ACLU investigating whether handover of individuals to ICE was unlawful.

## Legal Response
The ACLU of Colorado filed a lawsuit against Aurora Police Department, alleging the joint operation violated state laws prohibiting local police from assisting with immigration enforcement. The investigation is ongoing.

## Context
This incident occurred two months before the highly-publicized February 2025 ICE raids at the same apartment complex. The Edge at Lowry apartments had been a focal point of national attention during the 2024 campaign when President Trump falsely claimed Aurora had been "overrun" by Venezuelan gangs.
