# ICE Raids Durango Granite Business, Detains 5 Workers

## Summary
About 10 ICE and Homeland Security Investigations agents in five vehicles arrived at Rock Solid Custom Granite in Durango and arrested five employees who allegedly lacked "proper documentation." This was the first "real raid" in Durango since Trump's second term began, according to local advocacy group Compañeros. The business manager described the arrested workers as "non-criminals" and said his staff was reduced to just four people.
