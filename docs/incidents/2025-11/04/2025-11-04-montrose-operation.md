# ICE Conducts Operation in Montrose; Woman Detained, Vehicle Abandoned

## Summary
ICE conducted an operation in Montrose, arresting a woman and taking away vehicle passengers from a gravel lot near Pomona Brewing and Velocity Car Wash on the north side of town. A Chevrolet truck was left abandoned in the gravel lot. Federal ICE agents were on scene along with a Montrose police officer.
