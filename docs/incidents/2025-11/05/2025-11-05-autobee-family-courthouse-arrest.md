# Member of Historic Pueblo Family Arrested at Courthouse, Self-Deports

## Summary
Jose Guadalupe Alejo Lamberto, husband of Cristiana Alejo Lamberto (a member of the historic Autobee family prominent in Pueblo since the 1840s), was arrested by ICE outside the El Paso County Courthouse after his initial appearance for a DUI case. When his wife filmed the encounter and asked to see a warrant, an ICE agent stated they were "authorized to make warrantless arrests." The situation escalated when Cristiana called her mother to pick up her daughter. ICE broke the truck window and removed Jose. He paid $400 and signed an agreement to self-deport on November 11, 2025 - his 7th wedding anniversary.

## Historical Context
The Autobee name is prominent in Colorado history - Charles Autobee was the first non-Indigenous person to settle in what is now Pueblo County via a Mexican land grant in the 1840s.

## Impact
