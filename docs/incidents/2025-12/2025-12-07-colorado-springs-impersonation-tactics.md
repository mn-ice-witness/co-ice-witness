# ICE Agents Impersonate Store Employees in Colorado Springs; Detentions at Walmart

## Summary
ICE agents in Colorado Springs were documented impersonating King Soopers and 7-Eleven employees to approach individuals. On December 7, video was posted showing ICE detaining individuals outside a Walmart on Murray Boulevard. The Colorado Immigrant Rights Coalition documented escalating tactics in the area, including use of Automatic License Plate Reader (ALPR) surveillance to locate individuals. Community members organized volunteer networks to canvass neighborhoods and distribute multilingual materials in response.
