# County Commissioner "Boxed In" by ICE Agents After Filming Operations

## Summary
Routt County Commissioner Angelica Salinas reported that ICE officers "boxed in" her vehicle at the Oak Creek post office after she took video of their operations. She stated the officers "took off" when they saw her husband. ICE called her account a "blatant and pathetic lie" and released a photo from an agent's vehicle. Salinas stood by her account. The incident occurred amid increased ICE activity in Steamboat Springs and Oak Creek.
