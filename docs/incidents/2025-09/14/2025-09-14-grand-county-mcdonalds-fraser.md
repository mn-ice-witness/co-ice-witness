# ICE Enters Winter Park McDonald's; Legal Worker Questioned, Undocumented Man Arrested

## Summary
ICE agents entered a McDonald's restaurant in Winter Park, detained and questioned manager Justin Ramirez, who holds a legal U.S. work permit and was later released. Agents then arrested Evin Gonzales, an undocumented Nicaraguan man from nearby Fraser who had previously worked at the restaurant. Gonzales had no known criminal record. This was the first confirmed ICE activity in Grand County.
