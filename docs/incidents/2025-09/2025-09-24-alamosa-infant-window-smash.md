# ICE Draws Gun, Smashes Car Window with 1-Month-Old Baby Inside

## Summary
Jose de Jesus Aguilera-Bautista was driving with his partner Maya England and their 1-month-old son when three unmarked ICE SUVs followed them after he left an Alamosa County courthouse DUI hearing. Vehicles boxed them in at an intersection. An ICE agent exited with weapon drawn, pointing it at Aguilera through the windshield. A second agent smashed the car window with a baton while England screamed "We have a baby!" Glass scattered into the vehicle where the infant was seated. Aguilera was handcuffed without resistance and eventually deported to Mexico.

## Official Accounts

### ICE Statement
Claimed Aguilera failed to stop for police vehicles with lights on and refused to exit vehicle.

### ACLU of Colorado
Legal Director Tim Macdonald called the video "deeply disturbing." Attorney Hans Meyer said the force was "completely disproportionate."

## Legal Context
ICE is not allowed to arrest people going to or from a courthouse under Colorado's 2020 state law (SB20-083). The arrest occurred minutes after Aguilera left a court hearing.
