# ICE Raids Frisco Restaurant; 10 Detained Across Summit County; School Attendance Drops 35%

## Summary
Federal agents from ICE Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) and Enforcement and Removal Operations (ERO) executed a search warrant at Hacienda Real, a Mexican restaurant in Frisco, following an anonymous tip about undocumented workers. Two workers (a cook and a waiter) were detained and taken to the Aurora detention center. Agents also executed a warrant at a home in Dillon Valley and seized computer towers and employee records. The restaurant was forced to close. Over the following days, at least 8 more people were detained in the area through traffic stops near the Super 8 hotel and Shell gas station and at a construction site, bringing the total to 10 detained by September 26. Summit School District saw a 35% attendance drop lasting two weeks.
