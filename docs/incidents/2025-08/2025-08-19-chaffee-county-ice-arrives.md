# ICE Arrives in Chaffee County; Sheriff Confirms Presence

## Summary
ICE officers became active in Chaffee County. Sheriff Andy Rohrich confirmed "I can confirm they are in town" but stated he had no information about where they were going or had been, and was not given advance notice or asked for assistance. This marked ICE's first known presence in the mountain community.
