# Mother Arrested at Courthouse; ICE Orders Family to Bring 7-Year-Old for Detention

## Summary
Carolina Suarez Estrada, a 33-year-old asylum seeker with a valid work permit, was arrested inside the Chaffee County court complex while ICE officers were conducting operations in Salida. ICE agents then demanded the family bring her 7-year-old son Luciano to meet her; the child was detained separately in Alamosa. The family had work permits, legal employment at a construction company, and were lawfully present in the U.S. with their asylum case pending. Mother and child were held at the South Texas Family Residential Center in Dilley, Texas for nearly a month before being released September 15, 2025.

The family had work permits and jobs at a construction company in the Salida area. Despite having legal authorization to work in the U.S. and a pending asylum case, ICE detained both mother and child. ICE's demand that the family bring a 7-year-old child to be detained raised significant concerns about enforcement tactics targeting families.
