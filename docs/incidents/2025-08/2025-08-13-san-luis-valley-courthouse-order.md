# Judge Bars ICE Civil Arrests at San Luis Valley Courthouses

## Summary
12th Judicial District Chief Judge Amanda C. Hopkins issued an administrative order barring civil ICE arrests in courthouses across six San Luis Valley counties: Alamosa, Conejos, Costilla, Saguache, Mineral, and Rio Grande. The order stated that civil arrests "have a chilling effect not only on criminal defendants' presence at their hearings, but also on victims reporting crimes." This followed the earlier Western Slope courthouse order issued in April.

## Legal Context
This was the second such order in Colorado, following the Ninth Judicial District (Western Slope) order in April 2025. Both orders enforce Colorado's SB20-083 courthouse protection law.
