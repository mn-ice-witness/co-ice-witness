# Lakewood Man Arrested After Allegedly Ramming ICE Vehicles During Enforcement Stop

## Summary

On March 1, 2026, ICE officers attempted to stop a vehicle near the 200 block of South Ingalls Street in Lakewood. The suspect, Julinho Juan Lengua-Baltodano, 29, a Peruvian national, allegedly fled and rammed two ICE vehicles before barricading himself in his apartment. Lakewood police responded to assist with community safety after receiving a hit-and-run report. Lengua-Baltodano was arrested after authorities obtained a warrant. Despite initial reports from Lakewood PD of agent injuries, ICE later confirmed no agents were hurt. Lengua-Baltodano had been issued a final removal order on September 19, 2025, and is in federal custody pending criminal prosecution.
