# First ICE Visit to Telluride Residence Results in Unrelated Arrest

## Summary
ICE agents went to a residence at the Shandoka apartment complex in Telluride looking for a specific individual. When they didn't find that person, they made an apparently unrelated arrest of a man on nearby streets, taking him to the ICE facility in Durango. This was the first time ICE had come to a Telluride residence looking for someone. The Telluride Marshal's office was not notified before or after the operation.
