# Fifth Grade Teacher Detained with Family at Routine Immigration Appointment

## Summary
Marina Ortiz, a fifth-grade teacher at Global Village Academy in Parker (Douglas County), was detained along with her family while attending a routine immigration appointment at the ICE field office in Centennial. The school confirmed she had a valid employment authorization document through 2029. ICE stated she entered the country illegally in 2022. She and her family were sent to a detention center in Dilley, Texas, and eventually agreed to voluntarily depart to Peru in November 2025.
