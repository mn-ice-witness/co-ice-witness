# ICE Conducts Multi-Location Raids Across Denver Metro Area

## Summary
ICE conducted enforcement operations at multiple locations across the Denver metro area in January 2026, targeting approximately 100 individuals. Operations focused on apartment complexes in Aurora, Denver, and surrounding areas, with officials citing Tren de Aragua gang activity as justification.

## Sources
1. 9News (Jan 2026): [ICE operation Denver metro area](https://www.9news.com/article/news/local/ice-operation-denver-metro-area/73-6c341ee2-eae4-4d64-83c0-f6fc43b1c784)
2. Newsweek (Jan 2026): [Aurora Colorado Tren de Aragua ICE](https://www.newsweek.com/aurora-colorado-tren-de-aragua-ice-2026926)

- Cedar Run Apartments (Denver)
- Edge at Lowry (Aurora)
- Whispering Pines
- Jewell Apartments (Denver)
- 1451 Macon St (Arapahoe County)
- Ivy Crossing Apartments
