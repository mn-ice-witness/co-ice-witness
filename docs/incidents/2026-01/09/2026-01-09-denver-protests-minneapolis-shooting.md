# 1,000 Protest in Denver After ICE Kills Colorado Springs Native in Minneapolis

## Summary
About 1,000 people gathered at the Colorado State Capitol and marched through downtown Denver to protest the fatal ICE shooting of Renee Nicole Good in Minneapolis. Good, 37, was born and raised in Colorado Springs and was shot in the head through her car window by an ICE agent on January 7, 2026 during an immigration operation. She was a U.S. citizen with no connection to the ICE target. Governor Jared Polis called the shooting "deeply disturbing."
