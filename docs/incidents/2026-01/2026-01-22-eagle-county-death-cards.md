# ICE Agents Leave "Death Cards" in Vehicles of Detained; DHS Investigating

## Summary
ICE agents detained nine individuals in Eagle County - eight during what advocates called "fake traffic stops" and one while walking to a bus stop. Agents left ace of spades playing cards (bearing "ICE Denver Field Office" text) inside at least two vehicles of detained individuals. The ace of spades has historical associations with racism and death dating to the Vietnam War. DHS announced an investigation and "unequivocally condemns this type of action and/or officer conduct." Voces Unidas called it "psychological harassment."

## Historical Context
The ace of spades has been used as a symbol of death since the Vietnam War, when American soldiers would leave them on bodies of killed Viet Cong fighters. The symbol has deep associations with racism and intimidation.
