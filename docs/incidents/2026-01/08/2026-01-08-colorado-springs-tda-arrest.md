# ICE Arrests Alleged Tren de Aragua Member Wanted for Murder in New Mexico

## Summary
ICE Homeland Security Investigations arrested Yorvis Michel Carrascal Campo, a Venezuelan national and alleged member of the Tren de Aragua gang, in Colorado Springs. He was wanted in New Mexico for murder, racketeering, and drug trafficking in connection with a June 2024 kidnapping and murder.
