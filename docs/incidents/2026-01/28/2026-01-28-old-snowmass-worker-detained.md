# Worker Detained on Way to Job Site in Old Snowmass

## Summary
A worker was followed from their home to a job site near Old Snowmass on January 28, 2026, and detained by ICE agents around 7:30 a.m. The individual was transported to the Aurora ICE detention center. Voces Unidas de Las Montanas confirmed the detention and is providing legal support.

## Official Accounts

### Voces Unidas Statement
Alex Sanchez, president and CEO of Voces Unidas de Las Montanas, confirmed the detention: "There is an uptick in activity as of recent ... but only one person has been detained, from Old Snowmass."

ICE agents likely operated from the Glenwood Springs field office.

## Context
This detention occurred one week after the Eagle County "death cards" incident (January 21, 2026), where nine men were detained during fake traffic stops and ace of spades cards were left in their vehicles. The Western Slope has seen increased ICE activity in early 2026, with Voces Unidas documenting 120 detentions in western Colorado since January 2025.
