# DHS Statement: Grand Junction Arrest of Salvadoran National With Alleged MS-13 Ties

## Summary
ICE Denver announced the arrest of Juan Carlos Membreno Portillo in Grand Junction. ICE stated he is a known member of MS-13 (Mara Salvatrucha) and is wanted in El Salvador. He remains in ICE custody pending removal proceedings.
