# Durango City Council Votes to Consider Litigation Against ICE and DHS

## Summary
The Durango City Council voted to hold a special executive session to discuss potential litigation against ICE and DHS over federal immigration enforcement actions in the area. The council's move reflects local officials' concerns about ICE enforcement affecting Durango residents. No civil lawsuit has been confirmed as filed.
