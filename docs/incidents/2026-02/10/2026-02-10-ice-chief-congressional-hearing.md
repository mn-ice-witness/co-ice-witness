# ICE Chief Defends Officers Before Congress After Deaths of Two Americans

## Summary
Acting ICE Director Todd Lyons, CBP Commissioner Rodney Scott, and USCIS Director Joseph Edlow testified before the House Committee on Homeland Security in the first congressional hearing since the fatal shootings of Renee Good and Alex Pretti in Minneapolis. Lyons defended ICE tactics, declined to apologize to the families, refused to commit to requiring officers to remove masks, and revealed that only 3,000 of 13,000 ICE agents currently have body cameras. He also disclosed that new agents without law enforcement experience now receive only three months of training. Rep. Michael McCaul (R-TX) backed Democratic demands for body cameras, warrant requirements for private property entry, and restrictions on roving patrols away from borders.

## Key Testimony

### Todd Lyons (Acting ICE Director)
- "Let me send a message to anyone who thinks they can intimidate us. You will fail."
- "We are only getting started."
- Declined to apologize to the families of Renee Good and Alex Pretti; said he would welcome the opportunity to speak to Good's family privately
- Refused to commit to requiring officers to remove masks during operations
- Disclosed ICE has conducted 379,000 arrests since the start of the Trump administration, including more than 7,000 suspected gang members and more than 1,400 known suspected terrorists
- Denied existence of databases tracking American citizens, though multiple outlets have reported on DHS/FBI watchlists
- Regarding an agent who threatened to put an observer on "a nice little database": "I can't speak for that individual"

### Body Camera Disclosure
- Only 3,000 of 13,000 ICE agents currently wear body cameras
- 6,000 additional cameras being deployed
- Approximately 10,000 of 67,000 CBP agents have uniform-mounted cameras
- Lyons committed to releasing Minneapolis body camera footage: "I'm committed to full transparency. And I fully welcome body cameras all across the spectrum."

### Agent Training Revelation
- New ICE candidates without law enforcement experience now complete only 3 months of training
- Those with prior experience finish in 47 days at a federal training center
- Previously, training was stated to take over 4 months

### Rodney Scott (CBP Commissioner)
Criticized what he called an "unprecedented level of aggressive interference and intimidation" against federal officers, characterizing opposition as "coordinated and well funded."

### Bipartisan Support for Reform
Rep. Michael McCaul (R-TX), former Homeland Security Committee chairman, backed Democratic demands including:
- Mandatory body cameras
- Warrant requirements for entering private property
- Restricting roving patrols to border areas

## Colorado Connection
Alex Pretti's parents, Michael and Susan Pretti, live in Arvada, Colorado. Renee Good grew up in Colorado and her family still lives in the state. The hearing was held amid warnings that Denver could be the next major target for ICE operations after Minneapolis.
