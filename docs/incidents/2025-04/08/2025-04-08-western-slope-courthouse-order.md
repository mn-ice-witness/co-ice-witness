# Judge Orders ICE to Stop Civil Immigration Arrests at Western Slope Courthouses

## Summary
Ninth Judicial District Chief Judge John Neiley issued an administrative order directing ICE to cease civil immigration enforcement in and around courthouses in Pitkin, Garfield, and Rio Blanco counties. This was the first judicial use of Colorado's courthouse protection law (SB20-083) since the Trump administration began. The order came after ICE agents maintained a steady presence at the Garfield County Courthouse in Glenwood Springs since January, detaining 5-10 people either inside or just outside the building while perusing court dockets to identify defendants.

## Official Accounts

### Chief Judge John Neiley
Issued administrative order that ICE officers conducting civil detention in courthouses could be subject to contempt of court findings.

## Legal Context
Colorado's SB20-083 (passed 2020) prohibits civil immigration arrests at courthouses. Judge Neiley's order was the first judicial enforcement of this law since Trump's second term began.
