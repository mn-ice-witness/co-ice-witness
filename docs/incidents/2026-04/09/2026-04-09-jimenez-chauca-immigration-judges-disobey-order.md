# Federal Judge Releases Vicente Jimenez Chauca After Two Immigration Judges Disobey Habeas Order

## Summary
U.S. District Judge Gordon P. Gallagher ordered the immediate release of Vicente Jimenez Chauca on April 9, 2026, after two different immigration judges consecutively disobeyed his federal court order to hold a proper bond hearing. Gallagher had issued a bond hearing order on March 13, but Assistant Chief Immigration Judge Irene C. Feldman disregarded it at a March 20 hearing, and Immigration Judge Jennifer C. Whitko again ignored it at an April 2 hearing. Gallagher wrote: "At this point, the Court has granted Respondents two bites at the apple to prove detention of Petitioner was warranted. It will not allow a third."

- **March 13** - Judge Gallagher issues standard order granting Jimenez Chauca a bond hearing, requiring the government to prove he was a flight risk or danger to the community.
- **March 20** - Assistant Chief Immigration Judge Irene C. Feldman conducts bond hearing but fails to follow Gallagher's order.
- **April 2** - Immigration Judge Jennifer C. Whitko again disregards Gallagher's order at a second bond hearing.
- **April 9** - Judge Gallagher orders Jimenez Chauca's immediate release, declining to grant the government a third opportunity to comply.

## Context
The Jimenez Chauca case occurred alongside a broader pattern of immigration courts in Colorado failing to comply with federal court habeas orders. In the same week, Senior Judge William J. Martínez released asylum seeker Gurjant Singh after flagging that bond hearings had taken on "preordained outcomes." Magistrate Judge N. Reid Neureiter had released Jose Millan Olivas on April 2 for the same reason. The pattern reflects a growing confrontation between Colorado's federal district court and the executive branch's immigration court system.
