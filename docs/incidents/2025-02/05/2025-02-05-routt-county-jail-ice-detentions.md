# At Least Three Detained by ICE After Bonding Out of Routt County Jail

## Summary
At least three men were detained by ICE after bonding out of Routt County Jail in early 2025. All were taken to the Denver Contract Detention Facility in Aurora. The detentions included Darwin Mejia-Cruz (27), Victoriano Cayetano Leon (42), and Nelson Martinez Espinoza (44). Mejia-Cruz had prior arrests including allegedly firing a gun in the air nine times at Dream Island mobile home park in Steamboat Springs.
