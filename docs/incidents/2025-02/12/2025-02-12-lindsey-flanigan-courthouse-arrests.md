# ICE Detains at Least 3 at Denver Courthouse, Violating State Law

## Summary
ICE detained at least three individuals at the Lindsey-Flanigan Courthouse in Denver while they were fulfilling court obligations. One detained man had his 12-year-old son with him, and agents detained the father despite the child not having a guardian present. The Colorado Rapid Response Network confirmed these arrests violated Colorado state law SB20-083, which prohibits civil immigration arrests at courthouses.

## Legal Context
Colorado's SB20-083 (passed 2020) prohibits civil immigration arrests at courthouses or while individuals are traveling to or from court appearances.
