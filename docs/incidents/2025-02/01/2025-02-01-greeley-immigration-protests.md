# Hundreds Protest Trump Immigration Actions in Greeley

## Summary
Hundreds of people gathered in downtown Greeley to protest Trump administration immigration actions. Protesters marched from Lincoln Park to the Weld County Courthouse, carrying signs condemning mass deportation plans and ICE raids. The protest came amid widespread fear in the community following rumors of raids on the JBS meatpacking plant, which employs a large immigrant workforce. The predominantly Hispanic and Latino crowd came from Greeley and surrounding areas.

## Context

### Community Fear
Rumors had spread about potential ICE raids on the JBS meatpacking plant in Greeley. Sonny Subia, Colorado Director for LULAC, noted: "Well, here in Greeley, we're [hearing] that they're going to raid the JBS plant."

### Historical Trauma
The Greeley community carries trauma from the 2006 Swift plant raid, when 252 workers were arrested in the largest workplace immigration raid in U.S. history. That raid devastated families and the community.

### City Response
The City of Greeley issued a statement clarifying that police will not participate in immigration enforcement.
