# Immigrant Rights Activist Jeanette Vizguerra Detained Day After Protest

## Summary
Jeanette Vizguerra, a prominent immigrant rights activist named one of Time magazine's 100 most influential people in 2017, was arrested by ICE while on her lunch break at the Target store where she worked. She had spoken out against the Trump administration at a protest at the State Capitol just one day earlier. An ICE officer reportedly said "We finally got you" while arresting her. She was detained for nine months before being released on bond in December 2025.
