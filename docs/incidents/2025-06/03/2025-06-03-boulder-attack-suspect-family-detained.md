# Wife and Five Children of Boulder Attack Suspect Detained by ICE for Expedited Removal

## Summary
ICE detained Hayam Salah Alsaid Ahmed El Gamal, 41, and her five children — ages 4 (twins), 8, 15, and 17 — two days after her husband Mohamed Sabry Soliman carried out a firebomb attack on Boulder's Pearl Street Mall that injured 12 people. DHS Secretary Kristi Noem announced the detention in a video on social media. The family had no involvement in the attack; Soliman told police his family was uninvolved, and his wife voluntarily turned in his phone. Federal and state authorities confirmed no criminal charges were pending against the family. The family was placed in expedited removal proceedings and transferred to the Dilley Immigration Processing Center in Texas. A federal judge issued a temporary restraining order blocking immediate deportation on June 4, but a later ruling in July allowed deportation to proceed.

### Legal Arguments
Immigration attorney Eric Lee: "Individual responsibility is required for punishment or detention." He argued the family "should not be punished for his actions."

Immigration attorney Stephanie Izaguirre questioned whether the family met statutory requirements for expedited removal if they entered the country legally in 2022 on a tourist visa before filing their asylum claim.
