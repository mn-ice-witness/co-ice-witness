# 18 Protesters Arrested at "ICE Out!" Demonstration at State Capitol

## Summary
Thousands gathered at the Colorado State Capitol to protest ICE enforcement in solidarity with Los Angeles anti-ICE protests. Police deployed smoke grenades and pepper balls to disperse crowds. Eighteen people were arrested on various charges including obstructing streets, assault on police officers, graffiti, and interference with police authority. The protests were organized by Denver Party for Socialism and Liberation and the Colorado Immigrant Rights Coalition.

## Context
The protests occurred amid escalating ICE enforcement across Colorado. By this point, ICE arrests in Colorado had increased approximately 340% compared to the same period in 2024. The demonstrations reflected growing community opposition to federal immigration enforcement tactics.
