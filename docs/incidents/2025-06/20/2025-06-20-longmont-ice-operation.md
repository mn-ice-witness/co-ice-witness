# ICE Operation in Longmont; Rapid Response Network Responds; No Arrests Made

## Summary
ICE conducted an operation in a Longmont neighborhood seeking Jose Reyes Leon-Deras, an El Salvadoran national wanted for child rape in Italy. Colorado Rapid Response Network volunteers responded to the scene. ICE alleged volunteers "alerted the suspect and allowed him to escape" using a bullhorn to share rights information. The Rapid Response Network denied obstruction, stating volunteers only documented and informed people of their rights. ICE left the neighborhood at 8:28 a.m. with no one detained. Longmont Public Safety stated the FBI was also present for a "criminal matter not related to immigration status."
