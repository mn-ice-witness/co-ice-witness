# Man Violently Arrested at Federal Courthouse as Child Watches; Legal Observer Detained

## Summary
At the Byron Rogers Federal Building in Denver, a man was arrested "very roughly, very violently" inside the federal courthouse bathroom while his child watched, trembling and in tears. Additionally, a legal observer from the American Friends Service Committee was detained, handcuffed, and cited while observing court proceedings. The observer's charges were later dropped.
