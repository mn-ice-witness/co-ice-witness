# Soliman Family Detained Despite FBI Clearing Them

## Updates
- **Mar 17** - Colorado Sun [reports](https://coloradosun.com/2026/03/17/el-gamal-children-colorado-springs-dilley-detention-center-texas/) the family has now been in ICE custody for nine months. Family members describe moldy food, medical neglect, and weight loss of 20 pounds. An FBI agent testified the family knew nothing of the attack plans. The family remains at the Dilley, TX family detention center.
- **Jan 21** - Immigration judge [denies bond](https://www.cbsnews.com/colorado/news/family-detained-ice-colorado-bond-denial/), reversing September ruling. DHS called children "terrorist adjacent."
- **Sep 2025** - Immigration judge initially grants bond, finding family "not a danger to the community."

## Summary
ICE detained Hayam Salah Alsaid Ahmed El Gamal, 41, and her five children two days after her husband Mohamed Sabry Soliman carried out a firebomb attack on Boulder's Pearl Street Mall that injured 29 people and killed an 82-year-old woman. DHS Secretary Kristi Noem announced the detention on social media. The family had no involvement in the attack; Soliman told police his family was uninvolved, and his wife voluntarily turned in his phone. The FBI later testified the family knew nothing of the attack plans. The family was transferred to the Dilley Immigration Processing Center in Texas, where they have been held for over seven months. An immigration judge initially granted bond in September 2025, but reversed the decision in January 2026 after DHS called the children "terrorist adjacent."

## Affected Individual(s)
- **Name:** Hayam Salah Alsaid Ahmed El Gamal (wife), Habiba Soliman (eldest daughter), and four younger children
- **Age:** 41 (wife); children ages 4 (twins), 8, 15, and 17 (DHS claims eldest is 18)
- **Citizenship:** Egyptian nationals; asylum-seekers (arrived 2022 on tourist visa, filed asylum claim; visas expired)
- **Background:** Family was living in Colorado Springs, children attending local schools. No criminal charges pending against any family member. Wife cooperated with investigators and voluntarily turned in husband's phone. FBI testified family knew nothing of the attack plans.

## Official Accounts

### DHS/ICE Statement
DHS Secretary Kristi Noem on the detention: "We're also investigating to what extent his family knew about this horrific attack if they had any knowledge of it or provided any support to it."

DHS attorneys later argued in court that the eldest daughter Habiba and her teenage brother should not be eligible for bond because they were "terrorist adjacent."

### FBI Position
An FBI agent testified that the family knew nothing of the attack plans.

## Witness Accounts
Family attorney Eric Lee stated: "Now this same immigration judge has reversed his prior decision and determined that the family is likely to abscond" from justice.

Supporters stated: "Not only is there no evidence to support that the mother and children had any knowledge of their father's actions...they have proved the opposite."

Habiba Soliman, 18, described detention conditions at the South Texas Family Residential Center as "inhumane."
